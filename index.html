Youcefbenhamida

Youcefbenhamida is a personal automation tool that creates videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is operated by its developer and is used only with the developer's own Google account. The app does not offer public sign-up and does not collect data from other people.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the app accesses

When the account owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. The app requests only the permissions needed for this purpose and does not access any other Google user data.

How the data is used and stored

The authorization tokens issued by Google are used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel. They are kept in private storage controlled by the developer and are never published.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party, and it does not use that data for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
